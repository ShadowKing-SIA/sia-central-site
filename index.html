Recruitment division · Draft REC-001

Earn your place.
Learn the standard.

The proposed training route balances written knowledge with practical decision-making. Applications may be open or by invitation/referral, subject to command-staff review.

01 / INTAKE

Application & interview

Share a callsign, interests, and availability. Reception checks whether an application is complete and arranges an interview when approved.

02 / TRAINING

Cadet fundamentals

Proposed modules: agency foundations, communication, information security, intelligence fundamentals, teamwork and judgment, and anomaly awareness.

03 / ASSESSMENT

Written + practical

The final assessment combines a knowledge test and fictional scenarios that test teamwork, judgment, and careful reporting.

04 / REVIEW

Command decision

Command staff decide appointments, subject to The Creator’s supreme authority. Graduation does not automatically grant elevated clearance.
